Palette

Title-sequence teal, logo mint, ball-pit primaries. Yellow is the single action colour.

Type

Gochi Hand for the logo, headline and buttons (marker-pen look of the Nikki wordmark). Fredoka for body and credits (rounded sans, bold surname like the show’s credit cards).

Behaviour

  • 4 slides, slide left/right, 5s autoplay, pauses on hover
  • Arrows, ball dots, swipe and ←/→ keys
  • Ball pit sits under a teal scrim so it never fights the content
  • Reduced-motion users get a plain fade
